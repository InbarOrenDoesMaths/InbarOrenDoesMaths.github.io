About Me

I am a PhD student in the Institute of mathematics in the Hebrew University,
studying set theory, under the supervision of Prof. Yair Hayut. I am mainly interested in large cardinal forcings and in particular combinatorial results that occur by changing cofinalities of successors of singular cardinals.
I received my MSc from Ben-Gurion University under the supervision of Prof. Menachem Kojman.
You can reach me at inbar.oren2 'at' mail.huji.ac.il.
